# PayPal AI Hackathon: Rules & Requirements

**Event Name:** Build What's Next with PayPal and AI (PayPal AI Hackathon)  
**Host Platform:** Devpost  
**Source:** Hackathon Official Rules and Guidelines

---

## 1. Core Mandate

- **Fundamental Requirement:** Every submission must meaningfully integrate both **PayPal** and **AI**.
- **PayPal Integration:** Must meaningfully integrate at least one PayPal technology, API, SDK, product, or developer capability using the free PayPal Sandbox environment. Supported PayPal brand services include PayPal, Venmo, Braintree, Xoom, Honey, Zettle, Paidy, Hyperwallet, Simility, and Swift Financial.
- **AI Integration:** Must meaningfully incorporate AI into the application's core functionality or user experience. Any AI model, tool, or platform is permissible as long as the PayPal integration remains central.
- **Implementation Status:** Must be a functional working prototype or proof of concept (mockups, static prototypes, and non-functional demos do not qualify).
- **Entry Agreement:** Submission constitutes agreement to the Official Rules by the entrant and every team member, Sponsor, and Devpost.
- **Challenge Scope:** Global online event; no prescribed problem statements or tracks.
- **Eligibility:** Open to individuals or teams. No purchase or payment is required to enter or win.
- **Project Age:**
  - **New Projects:** Conceived and built for the hackathon.
  - **Existing Projects:** Must have been significantly updated during the Hackathon Submission Period. Entrants must document and explain the specific changes made. Significance of updates is determined at the sole discretion of the Sponsor and Administrator.
- **Third-Party Integrations:** Must be authorized under applicable terms, licenses, and conditions.

## 4. Judging Structure & Evaluation Criteria

- Sponsor and Administrator determine eligibility and judging methodology; evaluation may use expert panels, peer review, automated AI analysis, or a combination.
- Sponsor selects judges, who may be employees or third parties and may change. Judging may use multiple rounds or panels.
- Highest applicable overall scores determine potential prize winners, subject to eligibility and judges’ discretion.

### Stage One: Baseline Viability (Pass / Fail)

- Validates whether the project reasonably fits the hackathon theme.
- Confirms reasonable application of required PayPal APIs/SDKs and AI capabilities.

### Stage Two: Scored Evaluation

Submissions passing Stage One are scored across five equally weighted criteria:

1. **Technological Implementation (20%):**
   - Thorough and skillful use of PayPal Developer Platform and AI tools.
   - Evidence of genuine technical effort and non-trivial implementation.
2. **Design (20%):**
   - Delivery of a coherent, complete product experience beyond a raw technical proof of concept.
3. **Potential Impact (20%):**
   - Credible, specific solution addressing a real problem for a real audience, verified by the demonstration.
4. **Innovation / Idea (20%):**
   - Novelty, creativity, and distinctiveness from existing solutions.
5. **Presentation (20%):**
   - Clear end-to-end video demonstration.
   - Articulate problem statement, target user definition, and value proposition.
   - Clarity and ease of following the presentation.

### Tie-Breaking Procedure

In the event of a tie score between two or more entries:

1. Highest score in **Technological Implementation**.
2. If unresolved, highest score in **Design**.
3. If unresolved, highest score in **Potential Impact**.
4. If unresolved, highest score in **Innovation / Idea**.
5. If unresolved, highest score in **Presentation**.
6. If tied across all five criteria, the panel of Judges votes to break the tie.
